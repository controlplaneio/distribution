# Enterprise Distribution for Dex

[ControlPlane Enterprise for Dex](https://control-plane.io/contact/)
is a hardened distribution of the [Dex](https://dexidp.io) OIDC identity
provider for Kubernetes.

The ControlPlane distribution provides hardened container images and a Helm
chart, published to the ControlPlane Artifact Registry (`cpar.dev`).

> [!TIP]
> [Connect with us](https://control-plane.io/contact/) to explore how the
> enterprise distribution aligns with your unique requirements.

## Documentation

- [ControlPlane Artifact Registry](https://docs.cpar.dev)
- [Upstream Dex documentation](https://dexidp.io/docs/)

## Supply chain security

The distribution artifacts are built and published by ControlPlane with
signed provenance and SBOM attestations.

- [`cpar.dev`](cpar.dev) — main distribution channel
- [`ghcr.io`](ghcr.io) — deprecated legacy registry
