# Enterprise Distribution for SPIRE

[ControlPlane Enterprise for SPIRE](https://control-plane.io/contact/)
is a hardened distribution of [SPIRE](https://spiffe.io/docs/latest/spire-about/)
and the SPIFFE CSI driver for Kubernetes.

The distribution provides hardened container images built with FIPS 140-3
cryptography and Helm charts published to the ControlPlane Artifact Registry
(`cpar.dev`).

> [!TIP]
> [Connect with us](https://control-plane.io/contact/) to explore how the
> enterprise distribution aligns with your unique requirements.
